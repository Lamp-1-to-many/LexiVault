About & User Guide

Everything you need to know about LexiVault, its word structure, CSV import, and encrypted backups.

LexiVault is a portable, single-file personal dictionary built for multilingual learners. Every word you add is stored locally inside your browser's IndexedDB database (with automatic local fallback) — nothing is uploaded to any external server.

1. Word Structure

  • Word & Word Language: The term itself and the language it belongs to (e.g., English, Hindi, Gujarati, Sanskrit).
  • Pronunciation Guide: Phonetic spelling or IPA for each word.
  • Tri-Lingual Meanings: Dedicated fields strictly for Hindi, Gujarati, and English meanings/translations.
  • Category & Tags: Assign a category (e.g., Noun, Verb, Idiom, Daily Use) and comma-separated tags for instant filtering and grouping.
  • Optional Fields: Add a formal Definition, numbered Examples (1, 2, 3...), an Image upload (auto-compressed to save space), and personal Notes.

2. Organizing, Filtering & Navigation

  • Filter & Search: Filter by Language, Category, or Tag, or search across words, meanings, definitions, and notes in real time.
  • Arrange / Group By: Use the Arrange / Group by pills on the Word Bank page to list words in a collapsible A–Z Index dictionary view, or group by Language, Category, or Tags.
  • Edit & Delete: Use the compact action icons at the top-right of every card to view full details, edit, or delete.
  • Back Navigation: Browser Back button, mobile Back swipe, and desktop Backspace (outside text boxes) return you to your previous in-app page.

3. Bulk CSV Import Guide

Click Bulk CSV on the Word Bank page to upload a .csv file or paste CSV rows directly. You can download a ready-to-use template right inside the modal.

word,language,pronunciation,english,hindi,gujarati,category,tags,definition,examples,notes
  • • Separate multiple examples inside the examples column using a semicolon (;) or pipe (|) — they automatically become a numbered list 1, 2....
  • • Separate multiple tags inside the tags column using a semicolon (;) or pipe (|).
  • • Wrap any cell containing commas or quotes in double quotes ("...").

4. Encrypted Backup & Restore

Your dictionary—including uploaded images—can be exported as an encrypted .enc.json file using AES-GCM 256-bit encryption with a PBKDF2-SHA256 key derived from your passphrase.

  • Backup: Click Encrypted Backup, enter a strong passphrase, and save the downloaded file safely.
  • Restore: Click Restore, select your backup file, enter the exact passphrase, and choose whether to Merge with your current words or Replace all words.
  • Important: Passphrases are never stored. If you lose your passphrase, the encrypted file cannot be decrypted.
