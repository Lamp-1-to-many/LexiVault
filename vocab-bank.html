How Bulk Import Works

Paste multiple words at once or upload a .csv or .txt file. Put one translation per line using either | (pipe) or , (comma) to separate columns:

Word | Meaning Language | Translation | Note (optional) | Tags (optional, separated by ;)
  • Required columns: Only the first 3 columns (Word, Meaning Language, Translation) are required.
  • Multilingual grouping: Repeat the same Word on multiple lines to automatically group all its languages onto one card (and merge with words already in your bank!).
  • Topic tags: Use semicolons (;) to add multiple tags on a line, like Food; Travel.
